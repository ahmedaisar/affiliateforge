Why this direction

Picked by the owner on 17 November 2026. The comparison it was chosen from is in docs/product/decisions.md; the other shell was deleted rather than kept as a theme.

What it keepsWarm paper, serif display, a top bar, one ruled table, activity in words. The product reads as the trade it serves, which is the claim the generated stores make.
What it rejectedA left rail, a condensed grotesque, 39 px rows and a state bar on each row's edge. Those scanned better at thirty stores; A was chosen for what it says about the product, not for density.
What the pass changedRow density is a token (--row-pad); both banners use block children; the fold carries the screen's second block; the phone mock takes the full width at 390.
Untouched by the pickThe palette roles, the four states as word plus shape, the control heights, the 44 px touch floor, the focus ring, and every value in tokens.css.
