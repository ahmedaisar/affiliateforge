Phase 1 · design · 17 Nov 2026

Two app shells. One screen, drawn in both.

The screen is /stores — the portfolio, the one the operator returns to. It is drawn at 1440 and 390 in each direction, from one kit, so what you are comparing is the shell, not the content. Sample data throughout.

Both directions are light and both use one palette: a dark set is a decision for the design phase of the generated storefront, not for the control plane.

A — Trade press continues the proposal

Warm paper, serif headings, a top bar, one wide ruled table. The store is a publication and the product looks like the trade it serves: it reads as a desk with a ledger on it. Calmer at thirty rows; the weakest at a glance, because nothing shouts.

B — Workbench instrument, not publication

Cooler stock, condensed grotesque headings, a left rail, tighter rows and a status bar down the left edge of every store. The product looks like a tool that measures things: the portfolio reads at a glance, and thirty stores fit on one screen. Less distinctive than A, and closer to what the market already sells.
